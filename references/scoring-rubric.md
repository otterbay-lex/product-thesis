# Transfer Scoring Rubric

Score to clarify judgment, not to manufacture precision. Write one sentence of evidence for every score.

## Structural Fit — 1–10

How strongly do the source and target share the causal structures that make the mechanism work?

- **1–3:** Surface resemblance only; causal drivers differ.
- **4–6:** Some important structures match, but a core driver is uncertain or missing.
- **7–8:** Most causal structures match and the mechanism plausibly addresses the target failure.
- **9–10:** The same core causal chain is present; differences are secondary and testable.

## Transfer Distance — 1–10

How different are the domains in surface language, users, economics, regulation, and operating context?

- **1–3:** Near transfer; close category or workflow.
- **4–6:** Medium transfer; different domain with familiar operating logic.
- **7–10:** Far transfer; surface context is very different while a real structural mapping remains.

High distance is valuable only when Structural Fit is at least 7.

## Optional tie-breakers

- **Novelty (1–5):** Does the mechanism reveal a non-obvious alternative to current practice?
- **Leverage (1–5):** Could one mechanism resolve several linked frictions?
- **Testability (1–5):** Can the critical assumption be tested cheaply and quickly?

## Decision heuristic

Prioritize transfers with Structural Fit ≥7, explicit critical differences, and Testability ≥3. Do not promote a transfer solely because its distance or novelty is high.
