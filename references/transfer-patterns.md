# Transfer Patterns

These are mechanism exemplars, not product templates. Adapt only after validating the structural mapping and differences.

## Airbnb — making fragmented supply transactable

- **Surface Product:** Short-term accommodation marketplace.
- **Underlying Problem:** Valuable, non-standard private capacity was difficult for strangers to evaluate and use.
- **Structural Fingerprint:** `fragmented supply + standardization problem + information asymmetry + trust deficit + matching problem`.
- **Mechanism:** Standardised listings make supply legible; reputation, identity, reviews, and protections reduce trust risk; marketplace matching and payments lower transaction friction.
- **Transferable Principle:** When dispersed capacity is real but inaccessible, first create comparable representation and credible trust signals, then enable matching/transaction.

## Duolingo — converting distant value into repeated action

- **Surface Product:** Language-learning application.
- **Underlying Problem:** A long-term benefit competes poorly with immediate effort and distraction.
- **Structural Fingerprint:** `delayed reward + intention-action gap + cognitive overload + habit inertia`.
- **Mechanism:** Small tasks, immediate feedback, visible progress, and streaks shorten the feedback loop and make resumption easy.
- **Transferable Principle:** For beneficial behaviors with weak immediate reward, shrink the unit of action and make progress/reinforcement immediate.

## GitHub — coordinating work through visible, reviewable artifacts

- **Surface Product:** Collaborative software development platform.
- **Underlying Problem:** Distributed contributors need to make changes without losing context, accountability, or quality control.
- **Structural Fingerprint:** `coordination cost + handoff loss + contribution visibility gap + verification gap`.
- **Mechanism:** Versioned artifacts, explicit change proposals, review workflows, and traceable discussion turn collaboration into inspectable commitments.
- **Transferable Principle:** Where collaboration fails at handoffs, make the work artifact, proposed change, review, and decision history visible in one flow.

## Wikipedia — lowering the cost of collective knowledge production

- **Surface Product:** Collaborative encyclopedia.
- **Underlying Problem:** Creating and maintaining broad public knowledge is too large for a central editor alone.
- **Structural Fingerprint:** `collective-action problem + fragmented expertise + contribution visibility gap + verification gap`.
- **Mechanism:** Open contribution lowers entry cost; edit history, discussion, norms, and review distribute quality control.
- **Transferable Principle:** To mobilize dispersed expertise, reduce contribution friction while pairing openness with transparent revision and governance.

## Costco — simplifying trust-sensitive recurring choice

- **Surface Product:** Membership warehouse retailer.
- **Underlying Problem:** Buyers face too many quality/price trade-offs and suppliers have uncertain access to committed demand.
- **Structural Fingerprint:** `cognitive overload + trust deficit + search cost + incentive alignment`.
- **Mechanism:** Curated assortment reduces choice load; membership aligns repeat demand; buyer discipline creates a credible value signal.
- **Transferable Principle:** In high-noise recurring decisions, constrained choice plus an aligned commitment model can outperform endless selection.
