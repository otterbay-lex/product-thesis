### Six research lenses · 六个研究视角

- **User and context / 用户与情境：** Who changes behaviour, at what moment, for what job, under which constraints?
- **Value proposition / 价值主张：** What outcome changes compared with the actual alternative or doing nothing?
- **Business model and incentives / 商业模式与激励：** Who creates value, receives value, pays, or is subsidised? Which behaviour does the model reward?
- **Growth and retention / 增长与留存：** What brings users in, creates repeat value, or reinforces the system? What evidence supports that loop?
- **Constraints / 成立条件：** Which trust, density, frequency, regulation, data, motivation, labour, or distribution conditions make it possible?
- **Failure boundary / 失效边界：** What is the earliest condition under which the mechanism stops working?
